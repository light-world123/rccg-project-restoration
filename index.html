Our Story

A journey of faith, sacrifice and restoration.

The Calvary was planted in 1997 by graduating students of the Redeemed Christian Bible College. After several relocations, the church settled at 19 Abayomi Street, Oshodi, in January 2004 and grew from parish to Area Headquarters and then Zonal Headquarters.

“The building is the structure. The people are the purpose.”
Why Project Restoration?

A place built for worship — and for people.

The current worship facility has become inadequate for the growing congregation and expanding ministry activities. The proposed development is designed to provide a larger, safer and more functional environment for present needs and future growth.
